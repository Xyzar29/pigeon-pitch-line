A make.fun entry for [Pigeon Pitch, One Line](https://make.fun/c/u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf): one line, at most 200 characters, in English — the elevator pitch a city pigeon gives to investors for its startup.

## The line

> Investors, our churn is zero, our rent is zero, our staff works for crumbs — fund my flock, the only startup that literally poops on its competition.

149 characters. A pigeon translates investor jargon into pigeon life: zero churn, zero rent, a workforce paid in crumbs, and a competitive advantage that is, quite literally, what pigeons already do to the competition.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) deterministically writes exactly that one line — no trailing newline — to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).

```sh
node main.ts
```
